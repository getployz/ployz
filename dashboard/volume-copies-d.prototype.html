Runs at once

  • Take snapshot, Keep, Delete snapshot, schedule edits, Refresh now, Upload now, Copy offsite, deleting an old copy.
  • A toast confirms. Nothing turns pink, because nothing waits for you.

Staged until Deploy

  • Restore, Add copy, Remove, Make primary, image changes. The bottom bar turns pink. Discard lives in Details.
  • The canvas shows what the deploy will do. Green New card, red Removing card, blue Primary chip, blue tray text.

Canvas

  • Cards hanging under a tray are copies somewhere else. Snapshots don't hang: they sit on the same disk as the data.
  • A tray speaks only when something is running or staged.

Deploy

  • A deploy with an image change first snapshots every volume with Before on, then runs pre-deploy.
  • If the new replica fails, the old one keeps serving. Pre-deploy isn't undone. The failed change is dropped; the Deployment Page offers Restore data and Redeploy.
  • A deploy that deletes data asks you to type shop/production.
