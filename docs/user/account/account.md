title: Your account
description: Your profile, where you're signed in, and which emails you get.
---

Your account is you, not your organization. It's the same in every organization you belong to.
Click your avatar at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **Account** to open it. While it's
open, the avatar stays highlighted. The page has three sections.

## Profile

Your name, email and avatar, with each way you sign in, such as **GitHub**, and when you linked
it. They come from GitHub, so you can't edit them here.

## Sessions

Every place you're signed in, most recently active first: each browser, and each computer where you ran
`ployz login`. The browser you're using says **This device**.

To sign a browser out, click **Sign out** on its row. It's signed out at once, and the row
goes away.

A CLI session shows **CLI** and has no button. To sign a computer's CLI out, run `ployz logout`
on it (see [CLI and coding agents](../cli/overview.md)).

## Notifications

Which emails you get. See [Notifications](notifications.md).
